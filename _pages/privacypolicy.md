# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is a contract entered into between You ("you," "your," "yours") and *Zalaryx* ("we," "us," "our," "Zalaryx").

This policy applies to all information collected or submitted on the *Zalaryx* website (the "Site") and application (the "App").

We take personal privacy seriously. The ***Zalaryx*** application (the "App") **does not collect, store or share any personal information about you**. No account creation is required.

Personal **information shared with us via email**, or any other communication channel, **is never shared except to comply with the law, develop and improve our products, or protect our rights**.

## Information Collection and Use

Your content, e.g. saved calculations, is not saved on our servers nor do we have access to this data.

Data entered into Zalaryx resides only on your local device. The data is stored in an encrypted form on your device and cannot be read from or written to while the device is locked or booting.

Zalaryx does not require an internet connection to perform calculations. All tax computations are done entirely on your device.

## Email

If you email us for support or other feedback, the emails and email addresses will be kept. We will use the email addresses to reply to the original request, and we may use it for product updates.
